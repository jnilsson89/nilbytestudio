Jingle Drop · A Christmas game

Every chimney
is waiting.

Fly Santa's sleigh over a snowy village, drop gifts down the chimneys and dodge the shooting stars. Made for everyone from 3 to 99.

Coming soon to Android · free

How to play

One thumb flies. The other drops.

Drag on the left side of the screen to fly up and down, and tap the right side to drop a gift. When a chimney glows, a gift dropped right now lands in it, so even the youngest players know when to tap.

The full how-to

  • Build a streak

    Every gift down a chimney scores. Land them in a row and your multiplier climbs, up to ×5. Miss, and the gift just bounces off the roof: the streak starts again and nothing else is lost.

  • Watch out for shooting stars

    A twinkle at the edge of the screen warns you before each star flies in. Santa has three lanterns, and after a bump the team blinks safely for a moment. The first seconds of every run are star-free, and the village speeds up gently the longer you fly.

  • Beat your best

    Your ten best runs are kept on your phone, with the gifts you delivered and your best streak. Make the top ten and the game tells you which place you took.

For everyone

Made for little hands too.

  • A glow tells you when

    The chimney a gift would land in glows just before the right moment, so aiming is "it shines, tap!" rather than guesswork.

  • Gentle when you miss

    A missed gift bounces off the roof without a sound. No buzzers, no scary moments: calm Christmas piano plays throughout.

  • Never lost by accident

    The back button pauses the game, and so does a phone call or switching apps. Tap Resume and carry on.

  • Plays anywhere

    In the car, on a plane, with or without Wi-Fi: the game never needs the internet.

Gentle ads. No account. No tracking.

Jingle Drop is free, with ads from Google set for children: now and then a short ad between runs, never during play, and an optional video for one more lantern. A one-time Remove ads sits behind a grown-up check.

The game doesn't use the advertising ID and has no analytics and no sign-in. I collect no data from anyone, children included, and your high scores live on your phone.

Which also means there is nothing I can restore for you. Uninstall without a backup and your high scores go with it.
